EGG & WOOL 🐑🥚 — how to get it online (no coding needed)
What you get
A farm game where you hatch sheep, sell wool, upgrade buildings and battle your friends' teams (3 sheep each, turn-based). Accounts and progress are saved on the server (database), so you can log in from any device. Friends battle your saved team whenever they hit "Find a match" (battles are not live head-to-head, so nobody has to be online at the same time).
What's new in this version
Evolutions now grow like Pokémon: each stage is bigger and taller with one clear new feature, in the sheep's element colour. Fleecy gets horn nubs and a tail flame, Ascended gets curled horns and back spikes, Transcended gets huge horns with glowing tips, a bigger flame and wings.
Rarity detail stays clean: Uncommon gets a collar, Rare a gem, earring and sparkles, Epic glowing runes and a floor ring, Legendary light rays and a golden plume, Mythic light wings, Celestial a cosmic shimmer, orbiting planet and halo.
Ruin expeditions are no longer automatic. You choose which room to enter on each floor (upper floors stay hidden until you reach them, the boss unlocks last), and every fight is turn by turn: you pick the moves and targets. Rooms can be fights, loot, camps, spike traps and strange events (a glowing well, a lost lamb, a suspicious chest that might be a mimic) where you make a choice and take a risk. Each party brings 2 Hay Bales (heal a sheep 35%) and a Spark Tonic (+20% damage next fight), and finds more. Fleeing a fight costs health.
Dodging: a blue 💨 Dodge button next to your moves skips your attack. Until your next turn, hits on that sheep can miss (about 50%, more if it is faster than the attacker, less against hit-everyone moves). Every dodge gives ⚡ Focus (your next attack +30%), and a failed dodge still cuts damage by 20%. The game explains this in a pop-up the first time and in the Help tab.
Battle animations: melee sheep wind up, dash in and spring back, ranged sheep fire glowing projectiles, hits flash and shake with rings and particles, crits flash the arena, health bars leave a white trail, sheep sidestep when they dodge, and knocked-out sheep topple over.
Fixed: the Choose party button for ruin expeditions stopped working in the previous test version.
Ranked battles (new 🏅 Ranked tab): you start at 0 RP and RP come only from ranked matches, which are always live 1 vs 1 against another player (you need a full team of 3). One sheep fights at a time and the other two wait in reserve; when yours faints you choose who comes out next. Each round both players pick at the same time: Dodge goes first, then the faster sheep. Beating higher-RP players gives more RP, losses never take you below 0, and there are tiers from Bronze to Champion plus a ranked leaderboard. You can also challenge players who are waiting. Leaving a match for 25 seconds counts as a forfeit. Normal Battle-tab fights no longer matter for RP.
Shard pouch: tap the 👝 in the top bar to see how many evolution shards you have for each element and what your next evolution needs.
Prestige perk: after you prestige, every building can be upgraded to level 20 instead of 10, and past level 10 there is no farm level requirement.
Flock tab: new 🗑️ Select & release button. Tap sheep to select several (or use Quick select, e.g. "all Common or lower, not on team"), see the coins and shards you will get, and release them all at once. Special sheep, eggs and sheep on expeditions are protected, and you always keep 3 sheep. (Releasing a sheep that is out on an expedition is now blocked for single release too.)
Speed and cleanup (no features removed): the server now compresses the page and larger replies (the page goes from about 158 KB to about 52 KB), caches the leaderboards for a few seconds, and no longer re-sorts your whole flock on every request. In the game, long sheep lists only animate the cards you can see, sheep cards in lists use lighter effects, background timers pause when the tab is hidden, and the coin counter timer only runs while it is counting. Duplicate and unused styles and animations were removed.
Feedback: a 💬 Feedback tab lets each player send one message a day (Bug, Idea or Other, up to 500 characters). Messages are saved in the database next to the saves (a `feedback` table on Postgres, or `feedback.json` when testing locally) and anything older than 3 days is deleted automatically.
Live battle turn fix: reported as "it was my go but it said not your turn". Live turns have a hidden time limit after which the game moves for you, and your screen could keep showing your old buttons. Now every live and ranked turn shows a ⏱ countdown, the time limit is longer (45 seconds for live turns, 40 for ranked moves), the log says when the game moved for someone ("ran out of time and moved automatically"), the buttons grey out while a turn is being replayed, and clicking too late now refreshes your screen instead of leaving it stuck.
New owner-only troll sheep, "The Best Sheep In The Game": Extraordinary rarity (above Celestial), Cosmic type, a rainbow look with a 🏆, and a single move called Baa that always does exactly 1 damage. It can never come from eggs, quests or the Adventure; only you can give it (see "Giving out the Best Sheep" below).
Smoother animations everywhere: breathing, nodding, ear twitches, tail wags, better hops and jumps, bouncier hatch and evolution reveals, shockwaves and screen shake on big moments, wind-up attacks and hit recoil in battle.
Earlier: fixed clicking a sheep to open its details did nothing (a coding mistake in the previous update).
Battles: each battle can have a field (weather) that boosts one element by 20%. Stunned or burning sheep take 15% more damage, but a sheep that just lost a turn can't be stunned again straight away. Everyone can crit and dodge. A sheep with bond 50+ survives one knockout per battle. The enemy AI is smarter (it heals when allies are hurt and aims at weak targets). A turn-order bar and strong/weak tags when picking a target were added.
Icons: new inline SVG icons for elements, roles, stats, move types and battle status, so they look the same on every device.
Earlier update: deeper evolution (shards, bond, two paths per role, awakening) and a Research Lab tab.
Old saves keep working: new fields are created automatically.
Put it on GitHub
Make a free account at github.com and click New repository. Name it `egg-and-wool`, keep it Public, click Create.
On the new repo page click uploading an existing file.
Unzip the game, open the folder, select EVERYTHING inside it (`server.js`, `package.json`, `render.yaml`, `README.md`, and the `public` folder) and drag it all into the browser. Make sure `render.yaml` and `package.json` end up at the top level of the repo, not inside another folder.
Click Commit changes.
Publish it on Render
Make a free account at render.com (sign in with GitHub).
Click New + → Blueprint, pick your `egg-and-wool` repo, and click Apply. Render reads `render.yaml` and creates the game server AND a database for saves automatically.
Wait a few minutes. When the service says Live, click its link (looks like `https://egg-and-wool.onrender.com`). That's your game — send the link to your friends!
Updating later
Change files on GitHub (open the file → pencil icon → Commit, or upload the new version). Render redeploys by itself in a couple of minutes. Player data is kept.
Good to know (free tier)
The free server falls asleep after ~15 min of nobody playing; the first visit afterwards takes ~1 minute to wake up. Saves are not lost.
Render's free database is deleted after 30 days. Before then, upgrade it to the cheapest paid plan in the Render dashboard (a few dollars a month) to keep everyone's farms permanently.
Test on your own computer (optional)
Install Node.js, open a terminal in the folder, run `npm install` then `npm start`, and visit http://localhost:3000 (saves go to a local `data.json`).
Saves disappeared? (read this)
Render's free web server forgets any files it wrote whenever it sleeps or redeploys. Saves only survive if the game is connected to a database. If the game shows a red "No database connected" banner, it is not connected.
Fix A (Render database): In Render, open your Postgres database, copy its Internal Database URL. Open the web service → Environment → add `DATABASE_URL` = that URL → Save (it redeploys). Using New + → Blueprint with this repo does this automatically.
Fix B (free forever, recommended): Render's free database is deleted after 30 days. A free Postgres at neon.tech never expires: sign up, create a project, copy the connection string (it starts with `postgresql://`), and put it in the web service's `DATABASE_URL` environment variable instead. Accounts made before the database was connected can't be recovered; make them again once.
The server log says `(postgres storage)` when it is connected correctly.
Saving Render hours
The free server already goes to sleep after ~15 minutes with no visitors, and sleeping time is not counted. The game itself stops talking to the server when a tab is hidden or untouched for 10 minutes, so a forgotten open tab won't keep it awake. To switch it off yourself: Render dashboard -> your web service -> Settings -> Suspend Service (use Resume to turn it back on). Don't use "uptime pinger" sites, they keep it awake.
Reading player feedback
Feedback is stored as a sheet (table) in the database. To read it as a spreadsheet: in Render open your web service -> Environment, copy the value of ADMIN_KEY (a new Blueprint creates it for you; if it is missing, add an ADMIN_KEY variable with any long secret of at least 8 characters). Then open https://YOUR-GAME.onrender.com/api/feedback.csv?key=YOUR_ADMIN_KEY in your browser. It shows (and can be saved as) a CSV with time, player, category and message, newest first, which opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Without the right key the page does not exist, so players cannot read each other's feedback. You can also browse the `feedback` table in the Render database dashboard.
Giving out the Best Sheep
The easy way: open this link in your browser (it uses the same ADMIN_KEY as the feedback sheet):
https://YOUR-GAME.onrender.com/api/admin/give?key=YOUR_ADMIN_KEY&player=THEIR_USERNAME
It says "Gave The Best Sheep In The Game to ..." and the player sees it the next time their game updates. A player can only have one. To take it away again, add &action=take to the link. Without the right key the page does not exist.
The database way: in your Render database page click Connect and run this SQL (change the username, which is stored in lowercase):
update users set data = jsonb_set(data, '{sheep}', (data->'sheep') || '{"id":"sp_best","sp":"best","r":7,"heads":2,"ex":["galaxy","crown","halo","wings","orb","stars"],"wool":3,"face":0,"hue":50,"el":3,"role":1,"trait":"special","form":9,"name":"The Best Sheep In The Game","lv":1,"xp":0,"evo":0}'::jsonb) where name = 'theirusername';
About the sheep: it is a ★ special sheep, so it cannot be released, sold or awakened, it is hidden in the Sheepdex until someone owns it, and it does not count towards the 3 Celestial sheep needed to prestige. Its Baa always does 1 damage no matter its level, crits, type matchups or bonuses. When a player's battle team has an empty slot the game fills it with their highest rarity sheep, so expect it to volunteer itself.
